About This Game


Grapples Galore is a thrilling, physics-based, arena shooter focused on fast-paced grappling hook movement and active ragdolls. Use your grappling hooks to swing around, find weapons, and pull other players towards you! Grapple with your friends or with random players online!




  • Active ragdolls
  • Physics-based grappling hook movement (You can even grapple on to other players!)
  • 8 unique maps
  • 4 game modes
  • 9 different weapons
  • Online multiplayer chaos
  • Character customization
  • A wide array of match settings
