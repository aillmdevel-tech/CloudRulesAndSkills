About This Game

Fight your way through a post-war nuclear world by scavenging supplies, building your rocket, and taking on bosses! Explore a procedurally generated open world and find items to help you escape.

SCAVENGE

Explore each biome and scavenge materials. Biomes have unique structures for you to forage. Look for villages for extra loot and traders to expand your stash.

CRAFT

Use your materials to upgrade your gear, or craft helpful potions to help you survive the wasteland.

FIGHT

Each biome has it's own threats and bosses to take down. Defeat each biome one by one, gaining strength to take on stronger challenges.
